spatial.geometry.Rectangular(boundary=…) — what happens at the edge
A 10 × 10 domain, 4 × 4 patches every 4 cells: the anchor at (8, 8) overflows by 2 cells on each axis.
"drop"
default — the anchor is never placed; the 2-cell residual is not covered
"pad"
full 4 × 4 patch; overflow filled with nodata or pad_value
"reflect"
full 4 × 4 patch; overflow mirrored from the interior
"shrink"
patch clipped to the domain: 2 × 2 here, weights cropped to match
"raise"
split raises ValueError on the first overflowing window
